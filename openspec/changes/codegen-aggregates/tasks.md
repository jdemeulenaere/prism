# Tasks

Fixtures go under `crates/prism-cli/tests/fixtures/<spec>/`, one case per scenario, or per form of a scenario with several (`<scenario-slug>--<form>`), as the header of `fixture_tests.rs` describes. Programs are executable cases (`// run: <status>` with `stdout.snap` and `stderr.snap`); layout scenarios hold a `layout.snap` (task 6.2). Scenarios about `prism abi`, `--explain-cache`, `prism dump-clif` signatures or dumping twice are CLI tests in `crates/prism-cli/tests/`, not fixtures. Read every blessed snapshot against its scenario's THEN before committing it.
