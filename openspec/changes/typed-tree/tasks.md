# Tasks

Fixtures go under `crates/prism-cli/tests/fixtures/<spec>/`, one case per scenario, or per form of a scenario with several (`<scenario-slug>--<form>`), as the header of `fixture_tests.rs` describes. A typed-tree fixture also holds `typed-tree.snap`, the expected output of `dump_typed_tree` on its entry (D13); write it with `PRISM_BLESS=1 cargo test -p prism-cli --test fixture_tests` and read it against the scenario's THEN before committing it. Every group ends with `cargo test --workspace` passing and no existing fixture changing its expectation.
