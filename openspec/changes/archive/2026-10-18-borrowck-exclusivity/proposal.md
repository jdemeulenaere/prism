# Proposal: borrowck-exclusivity

## Why

Since `borrowck-moves`, the borrow checker rejects uses of moved values, but nothing checks that borrows are respected: `var x = 1; val r = &mut x; print(x); bump(r)` passes `prism check`, as do assigning or moving a value while a reference to it is still used, keeping a reference to a local after its block ends, and returning a closure that borrows a parameter. A closure that moves a capture even makes the enclosing body drop the value twice. This change, step 8 of "the road to the borrow checker" in `ROADMAP.md`, adds the exclusivity half of the borrow checker on MIR: any number of shared borrows or exactly one mutable borrow, each lasting until the last use of the reference that holds it (non-lexical), with disjoint fields borrowed independently, mutable receivers borrowed after the arguments, and closures holding inferred borrows of what they capture and never escaping. It implements "Stack-Scoped Borrowing and Mutation Rules" of `docs/architecture.md` §3 and settles the follow-ups `ROADMAP.md` assigns to it.

## What Changes

- **Loans and their lifetimes.** Every borrow in a body's MIR (written, implied by an argument, receiver, conversion, operator or default, or made by a closure capture) is a loan of a place, shared or mutable. Which loans each reference-holding local may hold is computed at every point, and a loan is live exactly where a local that may hold it is live (used later): borrows end at the last use of the reference, not at the end of the block. Copying a reference, wrapping it as `(&T)?`, reborrowing through it and borrowing a reference itself carry its loans along; re-pointing a `var` reference drops its old ones.
- **Conflicts.** While a loan is live, an access to an overlapping place is rejected when the loan is mutable or the access writes: a borrow or read that conflicts (`E5003` `BorrowConflict`), an assignment to the borrowed place (`E5004` `AssignToBorrowed`), a move out of it (`E5005` `MoveOutWhileBorrowed`), and a drop or end of storage, so that a reference never outlives what it points at (`E5006` `DroppedWhileBorrowed`, for locals and for temporaries). Fields are compared by path, so `&mut o.user` and `&mut o.email` coexist while `&o` conflicts with either. Each diagnostic labels the conflicting access, where the loan was created, and where it is later used.
- **Two-phase receiver borrows by evaluation order.** `v.push(v.size())` is accepted with no special rule, because MIR takes a mutable receiver's borrow after the arguments (`mir-lowering`); a borrow an argument still holds at the call conflicts as usual (`v.pushRef(&v.count)`). A safe call's receiver, which a `?.` test reaches, is borrowed right after its test instead, so that no argument can make the tested nullable `null` before the call (`n?.push(clear(&mut n))` is `E5003`).
- **Calls that return references** hold the loans of every input borrow they are passed (the caller's half of elision rules 1, 3 and 4, and a sound over-approximation of rule 2), so a `&mut` argument stays frozen while the result is used. A function value's result also holds the function value's own loans. A default value's generator does not keep the by-value earlier arguments it borrows. Checking the callee's side, narrowing rule 2 and `DanglingBorrowReturn` stay in `borrowck-elision`, whose boundary design D9 fixes.
- **Closures capture by inferred borrow.** A capture is mutable when the closure body writes it, borrows it mutably or lets a nested closure capture it mutably, and shared otherwise. Building the closure takes those loans, which last while the closure value is live; conflicting uses meanwhile are rejected. The modes are recorded in the elaborated MIR (`prism dump-mir --elaborated` prints `[&mut _1]`) for `closures` (step 17).
- **Capturing closures do not escape** (`E5007` `EscapingClosure`): a closure that captures anything, or a function-typed parameter, may not be returned (from a function, closure, default value or top-level `val`), stored in a struct field or enum payload, written through a reference or assigned to a captured variable. A closure without captures may escape.
- **Moving a captured variable inside its closure is rejected by the type checker** (`E4017`, "captured variable 's', which the closure borrows"), as `borrowck-moves` did for default values: `run { take(s) }` no longer passes and no longer drops `s` twice. **BREAKING** for such closures, which typechecked before.
- **Borrowed temporaries in branch values live as long as the value.** `greet(&u.nick ?: &"anon")`, which the `references` spec accepts, dropped `"anon"` at the end of the fallback while still borrowed. A temporary whose borrow is the value of a branch, a `?:` fallback or a block tail now lives until the end of the enclosing statement, or of the binding's block for a `val` initializer (`val r = if (c) &make() else &s`), dropped under a flag on the paths that created it.
- **MIR spans at the end of a block.** The drops and storage ends a block runs when control reaches its closing brace carry the brace's span, and those a jump runs carry the jump's, so "dropped here while still borrowed" points at the right place. Every `mir.snap` whose block-end positions change is re-blessed.
- Not in scope: the callee side of elision, `DanglingBorrowReturn`, borrows of top-level `val`s and recording a call's receiver operand (`borrowck-elision`); escaping, move-capturing closures and closure conversion (`closures`); precise per-field captures; a syntax for reborrowing a `&mut` into a local; lifetime extension through assignments (`r = &make()` is now reported as `E5006`).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
None. Borrow conflicts are reference rules, which the `references` spec's purpose already reserves for the borrow checker (design D1).

### Modified Capabilities
- `references`: adds "Shared Borrows Coexist", "A Mutable Borrow Is Exclusive", "Borrowed Values Cannot Be Assigned or Moved", "Borrows Last Until Their Last Use", "References Carry Their Borrows", "Calls and Closures Write Borrows Through What They Are Given", "Disjoint Fields Are Borrowed Independently", "Mutable Receivers Are Borrowed After the Arguments", "Safe-Call Receivers Are Borrowed at Their Test", "Calls Returning References Keep Their Inputs Borrowed", "Borrowed Values Outlive Their Borrows", "Closures Capture by Inferred Borrow", "Capturing Closures Do Not Escape" and "Borrow Conflict Diagnostics Show the Borrow and Its Later Use".
- `ownership`: adds "A Closure Does Not Move Its Captures" (`E4017`).
- `mir`: "Borrowed Branch Temporaries Live as Long as the Whole Value" is added, and "Borrowed Initializer Temporaries Live as Long as Their Binding" extended for borrowed temporaries in branch values, "Closures Are Separate Bodies" records capture modes in elaborated MIR, "MIR Is Well Formed" forbids moving a capture local, "Call Arguments Are Evaluated in Order" defers to the added "Safe-Call Receivers Are Borrowed at Their Test", and "Scope Exits Are Located Where Control Leaves the Scope" is added.

## Impact

- `crates/prism-borrowck`: new modules `borrows.rs` (the loan set), `places.rs` (place overlap and naming), `liveness.rs` (backward liveness of loan-carrying locals), `loan_flow.rs` (forward "may hold" analysis of loans per local), `captures.rs` (capture-mode inference), `conflicts.rs` (the access check), `escapes.rs` (the escape check); `error.rs` gains five kinds; `lib.rs`'s `check_file` runs the exclusivity checks on the elaborated MIR and records capture modes.
- `crates/prism-mir`: `CaptureMode` and `Body::capture_modes` (empty as built); `dump.rs` prints modes in elaborated MIR; `validate.rs` checks modes in elaborated MIR and forbids moving a capture local; `build/scope.rs` takes the exit span from the block's closing brace or the jump, and the lowering of `if`, `when`, `?:` and blocks schedules borrowed branch-value temporaries in the enclosing scope. `crates/prism-ast` and `crates/prism-parser`: `Block` records its closing brace's span; `crates/prism-typed-tree`: `ExpressionKind::Block` keeps it.
- `crates/prism-typechecker`: `check_move_source` rejects moving a binding captured by the innermost open closure (`TypeChecker` builder's `is_capture`).
- `crates/prism-cli`: fixtures under `tests/fixtures/references/`, `ownership/`, `mir/` and `diagnostics/`; CLI rendering tests; re-blessed `mir.snap` and `elaborated-mir.snap` files; fixtures and examples that relied on an unchecked borrow are rewritten (design D14).
- `openspec/specs/diagnostics/spec.md`: catalog rows `E5003` to `E5007`, and the `E4017` row widened to captured variables.
- `docs/architecture.md` §3: on archive, "Stack-Scoped Borrowing and Mutation Rules" moves into the specs. `ROADMAP.md`: step 8 and its follow-ups are settled, and the new follow-ups are added.
